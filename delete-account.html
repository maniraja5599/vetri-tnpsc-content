Delete your Vetri TNPSC account

வெற்றி TNPSC கணக்கையும் cloud backup-ஐயும் நீக்குவது எப்படி

An account exists only if you chose "Save your progress" and signed in with Google (app version 1.0.4 or later). Using the app without signing in creates no account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (Google account name, email, profile photo link and user ID).
  • Your cloud backup of study progress (XP, streak, quiz counts, bookmarks, revision schedule, daily goal and name).

Both are deleted permanently. We keep no copy. Progress stored on your phone is not touched; uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Vetri TNPSC → Profile.
  2. In Backup, tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: without the app (by email)

  1. From the same Google email address you signed in with, write to manirajankg@gmail.com with the subject "Vetri TNPSC - delete my account".
  2. We delete your account and cloud backup within 7 days and reply to confirm.

We only act on requests sent from the signed-in email address, so nobody else can delete your account.
