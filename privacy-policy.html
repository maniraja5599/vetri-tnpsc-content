Privacy Policy – Vetri TNPSC (வெற்றி TNPSC)

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Vetri TNPSC ("the app") is an independent exam-practice app. It is not affiliated with TNPSC or the Government of Tamil Nadu. Official information is available at www.tnpsc.gov.in.

Information we collect

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not ask for your phone number, email or any ID.
  • The name you type, your quiz scores, streaks, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app-interaction data to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice from the app's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Content updates

The app may download new questions from our content server. This request contains no personal information.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows a daily study reminder scheduled on your device. You can turn it off any time in the app.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) preparing for government recruitment exams and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all data stored by the app.
