Privacy Policy – Vetri TNPSC (வெற்றி TNPSC)

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Vetri TNPSC ("the app") is an independent exam-practice app. It is not affiliated with TNPSC or the Government of Tamil Nadu. Official information is available at www.tnpsc.gov.in.

Information we collect

  • No account is needed. The whole app works without signing in. We never ask for your phone number or any ID.
  • The name you type, your quiz scores, streaks, bookmarks, settings and any profile photo you pick are stored on your device.

Optional Google sign-in and progress backup (version 1.0.4 and later)

If you choose "Save your progress" and sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, stored in Mumbai, India) to:

  • receive your Google account name, email address, profile photo link and a user ID, so you can sign in on another phone;
  • store a backup of your study progress (XP, streak, quiz counts, bookmarks, revision schedule, daily goal and the name you typed) under your user ID.

Only you can read or change your backup. We do not sell it, share it or use it for ads. A profile photo you pick from your gallery stays on your phone and is never uploaded.

You can sign out at any time (your progress stays on the phone) or delete your account (see "Deleting your data").

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app-interaction data to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice from the app's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Content updates

The app may download new questions from our content server. This request contains no personal information.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows a daily study reminder scheduled on your device. You can turn it off any time in the app.

From version 1.0.4 the app can also receive alerts about important TNPSC news and newly added questions through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. For this, Firebase gives the app installation a random token; it is not linked to your name or email. You can turn these alerts off in Android Settings → Apps → Vetri TNPSC → Notifications ("News & new questions").

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) preparing for government recruitment exams and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all data stored on your device.

If you signed in: open the app → Profile → Backup → Delete account. This permanently deletes your cloud backup and your sign-in at once. Without the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.
